Connect to your Bee Flow server

Bee Flow is self-hosted: the workspace runs on your own server, and this app connects to it. Enter its address to begin.

The address you open Bee Flow at in a browser. On a standard self-hosted install that is port 5176; on your own network, http:// works too.

    Nothing about this computer is sent anywhere until a server is set. Bee Flow talks to the address you enter and nowhere else.

This policy explains what use of the trademarks is permitted without explicit permission, what requires permission, and how to ask for it.

## What you may do without asking

- **Refer to Bee Flow** in articles, blog posts, talks, social media, and academic writing — including using the name and logo to identify the project being discussed (nominative fair use).
- **State that your project is built on Bee Flow** or "based on Bee Flow", as long as your project is not branded as Bee Flow.
- **Quote the Bee Flow name** in package metadata for libraries that integrate with Bee Flow (e.g. `bee-flow-plugin-…`).
- **Use the unmodified logo** in slide decks, conference talks, and similar contexts where the use is clearly informational.

## What requires permission

You may not, without prior written permission from Bee Flow B.V.:

- **Distribute a modified fork under the name "Bee Flow"** or any name that is confusingly similar (for example "Bee-Flow", "BeeFlow", "Beeflow AI", "Bee-Flow-Plus"). Forks must be renamed.
- **Offer a hosted service under the Bee Flow name** to third parties. (Note: offering a hosted service under any name also requires a separate commercial license — see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).)
- **Use the Bee Flow name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership, certification, or sponsorship** by Bee Flow B.V. when no such relationship exists.
- **Sell merchandise** (apparel, stickers, mugs, etc.) carrying the Bee Flow name or logo.
- **Register domains** containing "beeflow", "bee-flow", or close variants, where the domain is used in connection with software, AI, automation, or related fields.
- **Modify the logo** (changing colours, adding text, distorting proportions, etc.) and use the modified version publicly.

## Renaming forks

If you fork Bee Flow and intend to distribute the fork publicly:

1. Choose a new name that is not confusingly similar to "Bee Flow".
2. Replace user-visible occurrences of "Bee Flow" with your new name (UI strings, docs, package names, README badges).
3. Replace the Bee Flow logo with your own.
4. Keep attribution to Bee Flow in `NOTICE.md` and source-code headers — that's required by the [Sustainable Use License](LICENSE.md), and it is not a use of the trademark in a branding sense.

## Requesting permission

To use the Bee Flow trademarks beyond the limits above — including reseller programs, "Powered by Bee Flow" badges with formal endorsement, certification programs, or commercial co-branding — contact:

**tomkooy@beeflow.nl**

Include in your request:

- Who you are and what entity you represent.
- Which mark(s) you want to use.
- Where and how you intend to use them (URL, packaging mock-up, etc.).
- The duration of the proposed use.

We try to respond within two weeks.

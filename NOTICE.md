### Scope of AGPL coverage

AGPL applies to the connector source as published in [nextcloud-connector/](nextcloud-connector/) and to any derivative work of that source. AGPL does **not** apply to:

These components are licensed under the Sustainable Use License v1.0.

### Why AGPL does not propagate to `server/`

The connector and the server are **separate programs** that exchange messages over HTTP using JWT tokens for authentication. There is no static or dynamic linking between the two. Communication is implemented in:

- [nextcloud-connector/src/server.js](nextcloud-connector/src/server.js) — issues outbound HTTP requests with `X-Beeflow-Source: nextcloud-connector` header.
- [server/auth/connectorJwt.js](server/auth/connectorJwt.js) — verifies inbound JWT tokens from the connector.
- [server/auth/connectorBootstrap.js](server/auth/connectorBootstrap.js) — handles the initial unauthenticated bootstrap handshake.
- [server/index.js](server/index.js) — registers the connector authentication middleware.

Per the FSF's interpretation of the GPL family ("Mere aggregation" and the "separate programs in pipes" guidance, [https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html)), two programs that communicate over a process-isolated IPC mechanism such as HTTP, sockets, or pipes are not considered a single combined work. AGPL §13's "Remote Network Interaction" obligation therefore covers the connector itself but does not extend to the Bee Flow server.

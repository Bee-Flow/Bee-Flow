### REQUIRED

1. **MEDIUM: the stacked layout may cost the table its semantics.**
   `agent-hub/src/marketing/sections/compareTable.css:100-106` sets `display: block` on `table`,
   `tbody`, `tr`, `th` and `td`. Some browser and screen-reader pairings (Safari with VoiceOver
   most notably) stop exposing a table as a table once its parts are no longer `display: table*`.
   When that happens the visually hidden `thead` (line 93) is not used as column headers. It is
   read as a stray line ("Bee Flow The alternative") ahead of the cards. Fix: give the markup
   explicit roles in `CompareTable.tsx:92` onward (`role="table"` on the table,
   `role="rowgroup"` on thead and tbody, `role="row"` on each `tr`, `role="columnheader"` on the
   `scope="col"` cells, `role="rowheader"` on the `scope="row"` cell, `role="cell"` on each
   `td`). That keeps the table semantics whatever the CSS does. Add one assertion to
   `CompareTable.mobile.test.tsx` that the roles are present (`getByRole('table')`, a
   `columnheader` count of 3). While you are there, add `white-space: nowrap` to the
   visually-hidden `thead` rule, the standard part of that pattern.

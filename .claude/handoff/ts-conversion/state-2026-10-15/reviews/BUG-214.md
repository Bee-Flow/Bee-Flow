# Review BUG #214 (Opus, read-only)

## Review of 7ae9f6164 (Opus, read-only, late round)

Verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED (small).
1. Medium: the truncation change breaks the breach register's deadline clock. `server/appStudio/templates/appBreachRegister.js:490` branches on `dateDiff(vars.breach.notify_due_at, now, 'hour') >= 0`. With `Math.floor` a deadline passed by 30 minutes gave -1 ("ran out 1 hours ago"); with truncation it gives 0, so for the whole first hour after the Article 33 deadline the screen says "0 hours left" instead of "ran out". Fix: branch on `dateDiff(vars.breach.notify_due_at, now, 'second') >= 0` (keep 'hour' for the numbers shown) and add a template test for a deadline passed by less than an hour. If installed apps keep a copy of the template's expressions, they need the same change (migration or a release-note line).
2. Low: `server/shared/expr/dates.mjs:71` `Math.trunc(n)` drops the fraction of a month/year amount silently (`dateAdd(x, 1.5, 'month')` = 1 month). Either document it in the catalog entry or refuse a non-integer month/year amount.
- Behaviour change for saved data, worth a release-note line: 'month'/'year' used to fall back to DAYS (they were not in `UNIT_MS`), so `dateAdd(x, 1, 'month')` was +1 day and is now +1 calendar month; an unknown or missing unit now fails (literal: at parse time, so on save/validation; dynamic: at evaluation). The shipped templates use only 'day' and 'hour'.
- Units: singular/plural/any case normalised; `normalizeDateUnit` uses `Object.hasOwn`, so `'constructor'`-style keys are refused. Calendar month arithmetic clamps to month end (checked Jan 31, Feb 29, negative months, year rollover); `wholeMonthsBetween` respects the time of day. No `-0`.
- Mirrors: `server/shared/expr/{dates,engine,functions}.mjs` byte-identical to `agent-hub/src/shared/expr/` and `mobile/src/shared/expr/vendor/`; `gen-shared-mirror.mjs --check` exits 0.

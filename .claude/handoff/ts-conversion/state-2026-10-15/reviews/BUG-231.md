## 16439f360: CHANGES REQUIRED

   `SiteChromeGuard` never see the data. Fix: guard every list there with `Array.isArray(...) ? ... : []`
   and skip non-object entries (same rule as `footerData.ts`), plus a test with the five inputs above.
